# ELDORIA-001 – Prologue (site's certified translation)

Image: `../documents/001-letter-1513.jpeg` (Spanish original, signed with an ornate rubric, likely Juan Ponce de León).

> March 1513
>
> Then rose the wind from the vast ocean, and with it were borne the white sails of a fleet away from the known land, like wings given over to the care of the unknown. The sea lay before them like a great volume yet unwritten, and the horizon, forged of restless waters and wandering clouds, was slowly consuming the certainties of mortal men.
>
> At the foremost prow stood a conquistador seasoned by years and trials. His eyes no longer pursued the empty laurels of renown, nor the passing favour of princes and peoples. Rather, he fixed his gaze upon that wavering line where the firmament bows toward the sea, for there, as the Ancients had written, lies an island that no compass can master and no map contain. It was said to be a sanctuary of the gods, where flows a secret spring whose virtue is to deceive death and confound the order of time.
>
> But beneath the gentle breath of legend stirred other murmurings, darker and more dangerous. In the stones of Jerusalem, certain learned men claimed to have discerned a sacred alphabet, characters of gold and fire, engraved not to reveal the truth, but to conceal it from unworthy and profane eyes. Further still, beneath the blazing sun of the lands called Yucatán, priests of ancient lineage had inscribed upon their stelae spirals and venerable glyphs, figures through which were set forth the slow revolutions of life and immortality.
>
> Thus, two worlds, separated by immeasurable seas, spoke to one another through signs and diverse writings, yet guardians of a single mystery. And not long after, the white sails were loosed from a nameless coast, guided not only by the course of the stars, but by the silent admonition of those marks whose meaning endures beyond the ages.
>
> There were those who held that such a crossing led to immeasurable riches and to eternal life. Others, of more circumspect spirit, whispered that it granted no such prize, but only a harsher gift, as wide and dangerous as the sea itself: that freedom for which no land shall ever suffice.

- "Yucatán priests, stelae, spirals and glyphs" → Maya numerals/glyphs → ELDORIA-003 (and the glyphs on 002 and 004).
