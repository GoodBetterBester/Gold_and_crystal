## Back – raw transcription (Yucatec Maya with Spanish words)
```
Le ken u jóok'ol le disco sagrado ti' le ja'o',
le iik'o' yaan tu jáal k'áak'náabo' ku péek ichil le úuchben tuunicho'obo'.

Te'elo', mix ofrenda mix k'i'ik'el, ba'ale' u t'aan juntúule' jach suficiente utia'al
u despertar le k'ujo'obo'.

Le pak'o'obo' kóomtako'ob, le ka'ano' mina'an u xuul, yéetel u juum le
ja'atskab ku núukik bey jump'éel yiik'.

Le úuchben máako'obo' tu ya'alajo'obe' le kúuchila' ma' u tia'al le lu'uma',
ba'ale' ti' le súutukil ken u péek le sáasilo' u yich le máax ku payalchi'o'.

Kaxt le kúuchil t'aano'oba', tu'ux le k'áak'o' ma' tu tóoka'al, ba'ale' tu'ux
tuláakal t'aan ku suut k'áak'.
```

## Our translation (unofficial)
1. When the sacred disk rises out of the water, the wind on the seashore moves among the ancient stones.
2. There, no offering and no blood: one person's word alone is enough to wake the gods.
3. The walls are low, the sky has no edge, and the sound of the morning answers like a breath.
4. The ancients said this place is not of the earth, but of the moment the light touches the face of the one who prays.
5. Find the place of the words, where fire does not burn, but where every word turns to fire.

## Reading
- Sunrise over the sea, low walls, site plan with walls on three sides and the sea to the east → **Tulum**.
- Precise point candidates: Temple of the Wind God (rocky point, top right of the plan) or El Castillo.
